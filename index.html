Anthony Chen

Hi, I am Anthony. I am a research engineer who loves building things and figuring out how they work.

Throughout my career I have been lucky enough to work on some genuinely interesting problems - training foundation models, standing up ML infrastructure, and poking at the edges of what large language models can do. The catch is that a lot of that work I could not talk about. So this site is my way of making up for it: a place to document the things I find interesting, write down what I learn, and share the side projects I tinker with in the evenings.

Think of it as a workshop with the door left open. You are welcome to wander in, read the notes pinned to the wall, and see what is on the bench.

What I am Working On

Foundation Model Training

Core model training of large language models for agentic coding use cases, including recursive self-improvement and reinforcement learning infrastructure.

ML Infrastructure & MLOps

Scalable training pipelines, optimized inference, and deployment systems spanning Kubernetes, distributed training, and GPU-aware scheduling.

Research & Publications

Published work on reward modeling, LLM evaluation, efficient compression, and cross-cultural bias analysis in NLP.
